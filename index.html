    The five packages

    You install one, royalegym[all], and it brings the other four along.

    RoyaleGym The battles your bot learns in. RoyaleSim The battle engine: elixir, cards, towers. RoyaleLearn The trainer that teaches your bot. RoyaleViser The viewer: watch training live, or a battle you saved. RoyaleImitate Start a new bot from one you trained.

    See It Play¶

    Your bot plays practice battles against an opponent, thousands of them, on your own computer. You decide what it gets rewarded for, and it works out the rest by trial and error. You don't need the game, a phone or an account, and you don't need to know machine learning: if you can copy a file and change a line of Python, you can train a bot.
