## See It Play

<figure markdown>
  ![A whole battle between two random bots, shown in the viewer](media/whole-battle.gif){ width="100%" loading="lazy" }
  <figcaption>A whole battle between two bots that play at random, in RoyaleViser. Yours starts out like this, and learns.</figcaption>
</figure>

Your bot plays practice battles against an opponent, thousands of them, on your own computer. You
decide what it gets rewarded for, and it works out the rest by trial and error. You don't need the
game, a phone or an account, and you don't need to know machine learning: if you can copy a file
and change a line of Python, you can train a bot.
