## See It Play

<figure markdown>
  ![A whole battle between two random bots, shown in the viewer](media/whole-battle.gif){ width="100%" loading="lazy" }
  <figcaption>A whole battle between two bots that play at random, in RoyaleViser. A bot that learns from nothing starts out like this.</figcaption>
</figure>

Your bot learns on your own computer, in two ways. It can copy people, from thousands of real
games: that's the way to a bot that plays like a person. And it can play practice battles,
thousands of them, and learn by trial and error from what you reward. You don't need the game, a
phone or an account, and you don't need to know machine learning: if you can copy a file and
change a line of Python, you can make a bot.
